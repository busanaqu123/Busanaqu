BusanaQu Posting

BusanaQu Posting is a content posting tool for BusanaQu, an Indonesian fashion (busana) online shop. It helps the shop owner prepare and publish product videos to the shop's own TikTok account. The owner signs in with TikTok, selects a product video, writes the caption, reviews everything, and then posts the video to showcase clothing products and direct viewers to the shop's TikTok Shop products.

The app only accesses the owner's own TikTok account, only with the owner's permission, and is used solely to publish the owner's own selling content.
